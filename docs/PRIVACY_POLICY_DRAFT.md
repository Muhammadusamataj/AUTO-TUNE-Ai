# AutoTune EQ AI — privacy policy (DRAFT for legal review; not published)

*Operator: [owner name/contact]. Effective: [date]. Applies to AutoTune EQ AI Full and Lite.*

**What the app does on your device.** It reads the title, artist and player name of music that is currently playing (Full, only after you enable Notification access) and applies a gentle equalizer to audio sessions Android allows it to control. It does not read the text of your notifications, messages, codes or account data, and it does not record or upload audio.

**Permissions.** *Notification access* (Full): used only to read media-session metadata. *Microphone* (Full, optional): used only by Android's Visualizer to draw a low-resolution spectrum on screen; no audio is recorded, stored or sent. *Internet* (optional): used only for the profile lookup described below. *Foreground service / notifications* (Full): to show that monitoring is running.

**Data sent off the device.** Only if you enter an HTTPS server address yourself: the song title and artist (and your IP address, as with any web request) are sent to **that server** to look up an EQ profile. Unknown songs are not re-requested for six hours. No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs are included. Clear the server address to stay fully offline.

**Data stored on the device.** Equalizer profiles you save or download, the server address, your choice to keep the per-app EQ experiment armed, and a short list of songs the server did not know. A diagnostic log is kept in memory only (never written to disk or uploaded); it identifies songs only by random per-run references, not by title or artist, and you can copy it manually if you choose to share it. Android backup and device-transfer of this data are disabled. Uninstalling removes it.

**Your choices.** Revoke Notification access or Microphone in Android settings at any time; clear the server address; stop monitoring from the app.

**Children.** The app is not directed to children.
